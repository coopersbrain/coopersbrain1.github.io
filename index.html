Amazon River Dolphins and Where They Migrate

About Amazon River Dolphins

Amazon river dolphins, also called pink river dolphins or botos, live in the freshwater rivers and flooded forests of the Amazon and Orinoco basins.

Migration

Amazon river dolphins move through connected rivers, channels and flooded forests as water levels change. During high-water seasons, they can spread into flooded forests to find food. As water levels fall, they return toward deeper river channels and more permanent water.

Why It Matters

Seasonal movement helps dolphins find food and use different habitats. Changes to rivers, pollution, fishing pressure and habitat loss can make these movements harder.
